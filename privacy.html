01

The short version

Effective for each user on the date they create their Subelo account. This policy explains how Subelo Distro (“Subelo”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you use subelodistro.com, the Subelo artist app and the public artist pages and release links we host.

  • We collect what we need to run your account, deliver your music to stores and pay you.
  • We share release details and files with the stores you choose and the delivery providers that help us reach them. That is how distribution works.
  • We don’t sell your personal information and we don’t run ads or ad tracking.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, export or delete your data at admin@subelodistro.com.
02

What we collect

Account. Your email address, sign-in times and basic security logs. Sign-in uses one-time email links, so we don’t store a password. Your plan, the date you accepted our terms, and how many releases and tracks you have used. If you invite team members, their email addresses and roles.

Artist profile and public page. Artist name(s), whether you are a solo artist or a group, your store profile links (for example Spotify and Apple Music), and any bio, banner, photos and social links you add to your public artist page.

Releases. Release metadata you enter: titles, language, release date and time, genres, lyrics, explicit and AI-use answers, store and territory choices, label and copyright lines, and UPC/ISRC codes. Credits: names and roles of the people on each track. Royalty splits: names, percentages and, if you add them, collaborators’ email addresses. If you add a collaborator to a split, you confirm you have their permission to share this information with us, and we may email them to confirm the split. Your cover art and audio files. Our review history for each release: automatic checks, notes from our team and status changes.

Royalties and payouts. Sales and streaming reports from stores for your releases, and the earnings we calculate from them. When you request a payout: your payout details (for example bank account or PayPal email), payout history and the identity and tax information needed to pay you lawfully, such as a completed W-9 (US artists) or W-8BEN (non-US artists) once your earnings reach the level that requires one.

Billing. Paid plans are not on sale yet, so we don’t collect payment card details today. When they launch, card payments will be handled by our payment processor (Stripe) and we will only keep the plan, amount, dates and the last four digits of the card.

Messages. Anything you send us by email or through support.

Visitors to public artist pages and release links. We count page views, store-button clicks and pre-save taps as daily totals, broken down only by country, referring website and device type. We don’t store IP addresses or any identifier for these counts and we set no cookies on these pages. See Cookies.

04

How payouts work

Stores report sales and streams to us one to two months after they happen and pay a few months later, so earnings usually appear in your account about 3 months after the streams. We update balances and pay monthly. Once your balance reaches $20 you can request a payout. On the Free plan you receive 80% of royalties and we keep 20%; paid plans receive 100%. If you close your account with a balance under $20, we pay it out rather than keep it. Full payout terms are in the Artist Distribution Agreement.

06

International transfers

We are based in the United States and our providers store data mainly in the US. Stores you deliver to may be located anywhere in the world. Where we move personal data out of the EU, UK or other regions with transfer rules, we rely on the safeguards those laws allow, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

07

Cookies

  • Sign-in cookies. Our login system sets cookies that keep you signed in and protect sign-in forms. The app can’t work without them.
  • No analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this page first and ask for consent where the law requires it.
  • Public artist pages and release links set no cookies and use no tracking pixels. Visits and clicks are counted as described in What we collect. Clicking a store or pre-save button takes you to that store’s site, where its own policy applies.

Because these are strictly necessary to sign in, we don’t show a cookie consent banner for them.

08

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile and release data: while your account is open and while your releases are live in stores, then for 24 months after you close your account or take your last release down.
  • Royalty, payout and tax records: for 7 years, as required for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Audio and artwork files: deleted within 90 days after takedown, unless we need them for a rights dispute.
  • Support messages and security logs: kept for 24 months.
  • Visit and click totals: kept as long as the artist page exists. They contain no personal data.

Stores keep their own copies of delivered releases and reports under their own policies.

09

Your choices and rights

See and update most of your details in your account and releases, and delete draft releases from your dashboard. Email admin@subelodistro.com to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it or your account, or to object to or restrict how we use it. We will confirm your identity from the email address on your account and reply within 30 days (45 days where US state law allows).

EU, UK and similar laws. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. If you are in the EU or UK and have a question about how to reach us, email admin@subelodistro.com.

California and other US states. You have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to access, correct and delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We don’t sell or share personal information in that sense. We won’t treat you differently for using these rights. You can use an authorised agent; we may ask them for proof. We honor Global Privacy Control signals the same way we honor a direct request, though as noted above we don’t sell or share personal information for targeted advertising in the first place.

Some data can’t be deleted while a release is live or while we must keep it by law, for example royalty and tax records. If we can’t complete a request, we will tell you why.

10

Security

Uploaded files are stored privately and served only to you, our review team and our delivery system. Our review desk is limited to Subelo staff, and only the owner can approve releases for delivery. Connections are encrypted. No system is perfect, so tell us right away at admin@subelodistro.com if you think your account has been misused. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you by email without undue delay, consistent with applicable law.

11

Children

Subelo may be used by artists of any age. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to our terms and manage your payouts on your behalf. If you believe a child has used Subelo without appropriate guardian consent, contact admin@subelodistro.com and we will look into it.

12

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the effective date at the top. For important changes we will email you before they take effect.
