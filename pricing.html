Add-ons · Paid plans

A few extras, add them when you need them.

Available on Solo, Plus and Label. Turn any of these on for a release from your dashboard.

Content ID

$3.99 per single or $11.99 per album, per year. Claims matching audio on YouTube and collects the ad revenue it earns.

Neighbouring rights

$1.99 per single or $4.99 per album, per year. Collects royalties owed to performers and rights holders when your music plays on radio, TV and in public places.

Keep Forever

$24 per single or $39 per album, one-time fee. Keeps that release live and earning even if you later cancel or downgrade your plan.
