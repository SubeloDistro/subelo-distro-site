How it works

Upload tonight. We take it from there.

Four steps, start to finish. No gear to learn and no dashboard to fight with — fill in the details once and we handle delivery to stores.

Start free
01

Upload

WAV or FLAC audio and a square cover image. Add track and release titles, artist and credit names, lyrics, and royalty splits if you are sharing the release with collaborators.

02

Automatic checks

As you upload, we check your audio format, cover image and required fields right away, so you can fix anything obvious before it ever reaches a reviewer.

03

Human review

A person on our team checks the release and either approves it or tells you exactly what to change and sends it back for another look. Plus and Label releases get a priority spot in the queue — see Pricing for the full comparison.

04

Out to stores

Once approved, we deliver to 250+ stores and services on your pick date, or only the ones you choose. Pick a date at least 14 days out if you want time to pitch for playlist consideration.
