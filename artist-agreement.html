Artist Distribution Agreement

The agreement between you and Subelo Distro for distributing your music.

First draft — not reviewed by a lawyer.

This agreement covers content ownership, royalties and copyright for every artist on the platform. Please have a lawyer review it before publishing or relying on it.

Who this is between

This Artist Distribution Agreement is between Subelo Distro LLC, 8 The Green, Ste A, Dover, DE 19901 (“Subelo”, “we”, “us”), and the artist, band or label creating a Subelo account (“you”, “Artist”). It governs the distribution of the releases you submit through subelodistro.com and the Subelo artist app. It works together with our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy; by submitting a release you agree to all three.

02

What Subelo does

Subelo is a non-exclusive distribution service. We deliver the releases you submit to the stores and services you choose from our current list, collect the royalties those stores report, and pay them to you under the terms below. We review every release before delivery; a release only goes out once Subelo approves it.

03

Your content and ownership

You keep full ownership of your music, artwork, artist name and any other content you upload. Distribution through Subelo is non-exclusive: you may also distribute the same music yourself or through another service, unless you have separately agreed otherwise with us or a third party.

04

License you grant us

To deliver your release, you grant Subelo a non-exclusive, worldwide license to store, reproduce, format, and transmit your audio, artwork and metadata to the stores and delivery partners needed to publish and maintain your release, for as long as the release stays live or until you remove it. This license ends for a given release once it is fully taken down from all stores, except for copies we must keep for records, royalties or legal reasons.

05

Review and acceptance

When you submit a release, automatic checks run first, then a person at Subelo reviews the metadata, rights information and content before approving it for delivery. We may ask you to fix something, or decline a release that breaks these terms, store rules, or the law. Approval by Subelo does not guarantee a store will publish the release; each store makes its own decision.

06

Royalties and payment

  • On the Free plan, you receive 80% of the royalties stores report for your releases; Subelo keeps 20%.
  • On paid plans (Solo, Plus, Label), you receive 100% of royalties.
  • Royalty splits between collaborators are set by the artist who submits the release. Subelo pays the submitting account; dividing payments among collaborators named in a split is between the collaborators, unless Subelo later offers direct split payouts.
  • Stores typically report and pay several months after a stream or sale happens, so your balance usually reflects activity from about 3 months earlier. We update balances and process payout requests monthly.
  • You can request a payout once your balance reaches $20, paid in US dollars with no payout fee from Subelo (your bank or payout provider may charge its own fee). If you close your account with a balance under $20, we will pay it out rather than keep it. Subelo may hold or reverse earnings that a store later claws back, or that come from fraudulent, fake, or otherwise invalid streaming activity.
07

Metadata and codes

You are responsible for the accuracy of the metadata you submit: titles, artist and credit names, genres, lyrics, explicit and AI-use answers, and copyright lines. On Free and Solo, Subelo assigns UPC and ISRC codes to your release at no cost. On Plus and Label, you may use your own codes.

08

Takedowns

You can ask us to take a release down at any time by emailing admin@subelodistro.com with the release title. Takedowns can take time to clear from every store, and some stores keep their own copies of reports and historical data under their own policies. You can delete an unsubmitted draft release yourself from your dashboard at any time.

09

Rights claims and fraud

If someone claims a release infringes their rights, or that your account was used to impersonate another artist or label, Subelo may suspend or remove the release while we look into it, and may ask you for proof of your rights. We may also suspend or close accounts, and hold or reverse earnings, where we find fake streams, stolen content, or other activity that breaks these terms or the stores’ rules.

10

Warranties

You confirm that you own or control the rights to everything you submit, including any samples, features or collaborator contributions, and that your release does not infringe anyone else’s rights or break the law. You agree to indemnify and hold Subelo harmless from any third-party claim, loss or expense (including reasonable legal fees) arising from your content, your breach of these warranties, or your use of the service, except where caused by Subelo’s own breach of this agreement.

11

Liability

Subelo works to deliver and track your releases correctly, but stores decide whether, when and how they publish music, and we can’t promise a particular result, stream count or income. Except for our obligation to pay you the royalties we actually receive and collect on your behalf, the service is provided “as is,” and to the extent the law allows, Subelo’s total liability to you under this agreement is limited to the royalties we have collected and owe you for the releases at issue.

12

Term and termination

This agreement applies to each release for as long as it stays live through Subelo, starting when you submit it. You can end it for a release at any time by requesting a takedown, or end it entirely by closing your account. Subelo may end this agreement, or remove a release, where these terms or the law are broken, and will explain why where we can.

13

Governing law

This agreement is governed by United States law as it applies to music distribution and copyright. Disputes will be handled in the state and federal courts located in New York, NY, and each party waives any right to a jury trial or to bring a class action.

14

Changes to this agreement

We may update this agreement. We will change the effective date at the top, and for important changes we will email you before they apply to new releases.

15

Contact

Questions about this agreement: admin@subelodistro.com, or see Support.
