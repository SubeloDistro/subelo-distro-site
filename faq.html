Plans and pricing

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Subelo Free costs $0. You get 1 artist name, unlimited releases at an 80% royalty share (Subelo keeps 20%), delivery to 250+ stores and services. Support is through the help center and email, with replies in about 2 business days.

How much do paid plans cost?

Solo is $21.99/year, Plus is $39.99/year and Label is $79.99/year, all billed yearly in USD. Every paid plan keeps 100% of your royalties and has unlimited releases.

What happens if I stop paying?

Your account moves to Free. Releases within the Free limits stay live. For releases beyond that, renew or add Keep Forever.

Which add-ons are there?

YouTube Content ID ($3.99/single or $11.99/album per year), neighbouring rights collection ($1.99/single or $4.99/album per year) and Keep Forever ($24/single or $39/album, one time). Add-ons need Solo or higher.

Releases and review

Who reviews my release?

A person on the Subelo team. Automatic checks run first, then every release on every plan is reviewed and approved by hand before it is sent to stores.

How long until my music is live?

Pick a release date at least 14 days out to be safe, especially if you want to pitch to Spotify editorial.

What if something needs fixing?

We tell you exactly what to change. You fix it and send it back for review.

Can I use my own UPC or ISRC codes?

On Plus and Label, yes. On Free and Solo, Subelo assigns the codes for you at no cost.

Stores and money

Which stores do you deliver to?

250+ stores and services on our current list, on every plan, Free included. You can send a release to all of them or pick some. See every store.

Do I keep my rights?

Yes. You keep ownership of your music. Distribution is non-exclusive. See the Artist Distribution Agreement.

How and when do I get paid?

Royalty statements and payout details are in your Sonosuite dashboard under Royalties.

Can I split royalties with collaborators?

Yes, on every plan, including Free.

Do you offer pre-save links?

Not yet. Every release gets a free smart link.
