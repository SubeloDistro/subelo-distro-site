Who we are

Effective for each user on the date they create their Subelo account. Subelo Distro (“Subelo”, “we”, “us”) is a music distribution service. We deliver your releases to stores and streaming services and pay you the royalties they earn. These terms cover subelodistro.com, the Subelo artist app (dashboard, account and release upload) and the public artist pages and release links we host. Subelo Distro LLC, located at 8 The Green, Ste A, Dover, DE 19901.

Sending your music to stores is also covered by our Artist Distribution Agreement. How we handle your data is in our Privacy Policy. By creating an account or signing in, you agree to all three.

02

Your account

  • You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email. There is no password, so keep your email account secure. Anyone who can open your email can sign in as you.
  • Use your real email and accurate artist details. One account per artist or team. Don’t share or sell accounts.
  • Any age may use Subelo. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms on your behalf.
  • You are responsible for what happens in your account, including releases submitted from it.
03

Plans and prices

Plans, prices and limits are listed on our pricing page. Prices are in US dollars and billed yearly.

  • Free costs $0. It includes unlimited releases, an 80% royalty share (Subelo keeps 20%), and delivery to all stores on our current list.
  • Paid plans (Solo, Plus, Label) deliver to the same stores and keep 100% of royalties. Paid plans are not on sale yet. Choosing a paid plan in the app today does not charge you or change your account.
  • Billing, renewals, cancellations and refunds: billing will be handled through our payment processor (Stripe). You may cancel or change your plan at any time. Refunds are considered case by case. Where a plan auto-renews, we will email you a reminder before the renewal date and follow the cancellation notice periods required in your state.
  • We may change prices or plans in the future. If a change affects a plan you pay for, we will tell you before it takes effect.
04

Royalties and payouts

Stores report and pay royalties several months after streams and sales happen, so earnings usually show in your account about 3 months later. We update balances and pay monthly. You can request a payout once your balance reaches $20. Payouts are made in US dollars with no payout fee from Subelo; your bank or payout provider may charge its own fee. If you close your account with a balance under $20, we will pay it out rather than keep it. We may hold or reverse earnings that stores later claw back, or that come from fraudulent or fake streaming activity. Full payout terms are in the Artist Distribution Agreement.

05

Uploading and releasing music

When you submit a release, automatic checks run first, then a person at Subelo reviews it and the owner approves it before we deliver it to stores. We may ask you to fix things or decline a release. The details, including content rules, royalties and takedowns, are in the Artist Distribution Agreement.

06

Using Subelo fairly

Don’t use Subelo to:

  • upload music, artwork or names you don’t have the rights to;
  • pretend to be another artist, person or label;
  • buy or fake streams, or break the law or the rules of the stores we deliver to;
  • get into other people’s accounts, or try to break, overload or scrape the site;
  • send spam or harmful files.

We may pause or close accounts that do these things, and hold earnings tied to them.

Our site and brand

08

Closing your account

You can stop using Subelo at any time. To close your account, email admin@subelodistro.com. You can delete unsubmitted draft releases yourself from your dashboard. What happens to releases that are already live is covered in the agreement.

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or the agreement. Where we can, we will tell you why first.

We may update these terms. We will change the effective date at the top, and for important changes we will email you before they apply.
