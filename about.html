What Subelo does

Subelo Distro gets independent artists’ music into stores like Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube Music and TikTok. You upload your release once, and we deliver it to 250+ stores and services on our current list, on every plan, Free included (see the list).

02

What makes it different

  • A person checks every release. Automatic checks catch the technical problems, then our desk reviews the details and rights, and Jose approves every release by hand before it goes out. If something needs fixing, we tell you exactly what.
  • A real free plan. Free costs $0, reaches the same stores as paid plans, and you keep 80% of royalties. Paid plans keep 100%.
  • Simple, lower prices. See our Pricing page for the full breakdown.
